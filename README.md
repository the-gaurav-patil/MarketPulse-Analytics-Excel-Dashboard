# 📊 MarketPulse Analytics Dashboard

**MarketPulse Analytics** is an interactive business intelligence dashboard designed to analyze and monitor **sales performance, profitability, expenses, customer behavior, regional performance, product categories, and operational trends**.

The project transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights through an interactive dashboard, helping stakeholders understand revenue drivers, profitability trends, customer segments, and areas requiring improvement.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The primary objective of this project is to build a comprehensive analytics solution that enables businesses to:

- Monitor overall revenue, expenses, profit, and sales quantity
- Analyze monthly sales and profit trends
- Evaluate regional revenue performance
- Identify high-performing product categories
- Understand customer and customer-type contribution
- Analyze expense and profit margins
- Compare new vs. returning customer performance
- Identify opportunities for improving profitability and revenue growth

---

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

### Revenue & Profitability
The business generated approximately **$401.7K in revenue** and **$144.2K in profit**, resulting in a healthy **36% profit margin**.

### Monthly Performance
Monthly revenue fluctuates throughout the year, with **February recording the highest revenue at approximately $46.5K**. October recorded the lowest monthly revenue at around **$22.4K**, highlighting an opportunity to strengthen sales during weaker periods.

### Regional Performance
The **West region contributes the highest revenue share at 32%**, followed by South at 25%, Northeast at 24%, and Midwest at 18%.

### Product Category Performance
**Technology is the highest-performing category with approximately $104.4K in revenue**, followed by Accessories at $89.2K and Office at $88.3K.

### Customer Performance
Returning customers generated approximately **$247.4K**, significantly exceeding the **$154.3K generated by new customers**, demonstrating strong customer retention and repeat business.

### Customer Type
**Retail customers contribute the largest share of revenue at 40%**, generating approximately $161.5K, followed by Corporate, Consumer, and Enterprise customers.
